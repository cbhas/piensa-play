# PiensaPlay — three-minute pitch script

Target duration: 2:40–2:55. Record the app itself; avoid long talking-head sections.

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

**Visual:** A child receives a dramatic school message and reaches for Share.

**Voice:** “For a child, one tap can spread a false alert, a manipulated image or harm to a classmate. Telling young people to ‘be careful’ is not enough. They need a habit they can practise before the next viral post arrives.”

## 0:25–0:45 — The promise

**Visual:** PiensaPlay welcome screen, language switch and Digital City.

**Voice:** “PiensaPlay is a bilingual, offline-capable media and information literacy game for ages eight to twelve. Players restore trust in a Digital City by making evidence-based choices.”

## 0:45–1:30 — Show the experience

**Visual:** Open mission one, reveal clues, choose an option and show feedback.

**Voice:** “Every mission teaches PIENSA: Pause, Identify, Examine, Notice, Seek and Act. Instead of memorizing a definition, the learner investigates a realistic scenario, sees why each choice matters and carries a clear action into real life.”

**Visual:** Brief cuts of the AI image and sharing-ripple missions.

**Voice:** “The flagship prototype tackles source verification, AI-generated media and the social consequences of sharing.”

## 1:30–1:55 — Inclusion and real-world delivery

**Visual:** English/Spanish, larger text, reduced motion and classroom mode.

**Voice:** “It works in Spanish and English, supports larger text and reduced motion, and needs no ads or real name. Classroom mode lets a facilitator turn one shared screen into a debate, vote and evidence check—even when every learner does not own a device.”

## 1:55–2:20 — Evidence

**Visual:** Baseline prompt, learning-impact card and facilitator guide.

**Voice:** “PiensaPlay measures anonymous baseline and transfer decisions, not attention or personal profiles. That gives facilitators a responsible way to improve the learning design. Our next milestone is a supervised pilot; we will report evidence, not invented impact claims.”

## 2:20–2:45 — Feasibility and scale

**Visual:** Flutter platforms, offline badge and content cards.

**Voice:** “One Flutter codebase reaches Android, iOS and web. Missions are modular, so communities can co-design culturally relevant scenarios and validated language packs. The model can scale through schools, libraries and youth organizations without behavioural advertising.”

## 2:45–2:55 — Close

**Visual:** Trust restored in the city; team together.

**Voice:** “Information shapes communities. PiensaPlay helps every child pause, think and play their part.”

## Recording notes

- Use subtitles throughout and check that text remains readable on a phone.
- Show a real interaction within the first 45 seconds.
- Include all team members briefly and state each role on screen.
- Replace any prototype statistic with verified pilot data or label it as a target.
- End with a short URL or QR code tested on a fresh device.
